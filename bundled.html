The Agent Pride Architecture

A multi-agent ecosystem orchestrated with strict boundaries and seamless handoffs.

🦁

Scout Agent

Financial Literacy Coach

  • RANK: Max 3 SMS/day. Never recommends loans.
  • TRAIL: Uses harvest calendar context.
  • HUNT: Escalates on "financial stress" triggers.
🛡️

Guardian Agent

Loan Triage Analyst

  • RANK: Approves <= KES 15K. Needs 3+ risk flags to deny.
  • TRAIL: Maps transaction history to district baselines.
  • HUNT: Escalates complex cases to Hunter.
🎯

Hunter Agent

Human-in-Loop Coordinator

  • RANK: NEVER approves/denies. Preps briefings only.
  • TRAIL: Knows human officer specialty areas.
  • HUNT: Prepares packet for final human decision.

Ethical Architecture & Data Sovereignty

Dignity-centered design respecting the Kenya DPA 2022.

ETHOS & TRACK Frameworks

We've eliminated urban-centric biases that penalized erratic cash flows, actively monitoring algorithmic bias against vulnerable groups.

OASIS Protocol

100% of member data remains under African governance (AWS Africa Region). Strict k-anonymity protects member privacy in rural villages.

PRIDE Loop Oversight

Mandatory human review for large loans (>50K KES) or vulnerable profiles (e.g., households with young children).
